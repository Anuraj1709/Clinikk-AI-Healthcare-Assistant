Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

How does membership work? 

Pay a monthly or annual fee and get unlimited doctor visits, free medicines, free lab tests, and optional health insurance — all in one plan. Walk into any Health Hub anytime.

 What services can I get at Clinikk? 

Unlimited OPD consultations, prescribed medicines and tests at zero cost, specialist referral reimbursements, 24/7 teleconsult, and cashless hospitalization (insurance add-on).

 Is Clinikk a health insurance company? 

No. Clinikk is a primary care provider. Health insurance is an optional add-on provided in partnership with IndusInd General Insurance, an IRDAI-registered company.

 What if I am outside Bangalore? 

Our Health Hubs are currently in Bengaluru. However, our 24/7 teleconsult is available pan-India through the Clinikk app.
