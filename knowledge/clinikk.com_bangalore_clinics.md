### Acute Conditions

We provide effective treatment for acute conditions such as fever, cold, cough, sore throat, and headaches. Our doctors utilize the latest medical guidelines and treatments to ensure quick and effective relief from these common ailments.

### Chronic Diseases

Our clinics support ongoing care for diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, and other chronic diseases through regular monitoring, medication management, and lifestyle guidance.

### Respiratory Infections

Clinikk doctors diagnose and treat common respiratory infections with guideline-led care, follow-ups, and referral support whenever advanced care is needed.

### Skin Conditions

Get help for acne, dandruff, allergic reactions, fungal infections, dermatitis, and other common skin concerns at your nearest Health Hub.

### Infections

Our Health Hubs manage stomach infections, diarrhea, wound infections, urinary tract infections, and related concerns with careful diagnosis and treatment.

### Pain Management

For body aches, back pain, joint pain, and everyday discomfort, our doctors help identify causes and create a practical plan for recovery.

### Dengue Management

Clinikk Health Hubs help monitor symptoms, guide lab testing, and support recovery for dengue and fever-related concerns.

### Lab Testing Services

Most clinics support common lab tests and routine monitoring so patients can get consultation and essential diagnostics in one place.

What types of medical consultations are available at Clinikk Health Hubs? 

Clinikk Health Hubs provide general physician consultations for everyday illnesses, chronic disease follow-ups, infections, pain, skin concerns, and preventive health needs.

 How do I book an appointment at Clinikk clinics? 

You can walk in directly or book a visit from the clinic card. Most Health Hubs are open Monday to Saturday during morning and evening sessions.

 What are the clinic timings and days? 

The usual timings are Monday to Saturday, 9:30 AM to 1 PM and 4 PM to 8:30 PM. Calling the nearest hub before visiting is recommended.

 Can I get lab tests done at your Health Hub? 

Yes, common lab tests and routine monitoring are available at Clinikk Health Hubs. Advanced imaging may need a referral to a partner facility.

 What types of diagnostic imaging do you provide? 

Clinikk Health Hubs do not provide X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, or ultrasounds onsite. Doctors can guide you to the right facility if imaging is required.
