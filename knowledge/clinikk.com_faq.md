Frequently Asked Questions
==========================

What types of medical consultations are available at Clinikk Health Hubs? 

We offer consultations for common conditions like fever, body aches, diabetes, and more. Our general physicians are trained in family medicine, primary care, and urgent care.

 Can I receive vaccinations at Clinikk clinics? 

We do not stock vaccinations, but we can advise you on what vaccinations are necessary and administer vaccinations you bring from outside.

 Do you offer allergy testing and treatment at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, allergy testing and treatment are provided at our Health Hubs.

 Can I get a health screening done at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, we offer health screenings including blood tests, cholesterol checks, and more.

 What types of diagnostic imaging do you provide? 

We do not offer diagnostic imaging like X-rays or MRIs; however, basic lab tests are available at all our Health Hubs.

 Do Clinikk clinics provide dermatological services? 

Dedicated dermatological consultations are not available, but our general physicians can assess and treat a wide range of common skin conditions.

 Are mental health services available at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, our physicians are trained to handle mental health concerns and can provide initial support and referrals to mental health professionals.

 Can I receive treatment for chronic diseases at Clinikk clinics? 

Absolutely. We provide comprehensive management plans for chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension, focusing on medication management, lifestyle advice, and regular monitoring.

 How do I book an appointment at Clinikk clinics? 

You can book an appointment by visiting clinikk.com or by calling your nearest Clinikk Health Hub. We also welcome walk-ins with zero wait time.

 What are the clinic timings and days? 

Our clinics operate Monday to Saturday: Morning 9:30AM–1:00PM and Evening 4:00PM–8:30PM. All clinics are closed on Sundays. Hours may vary — we advise calling ahead.

 What types of lab tests can be done at your Health Hub? 

We offer common blood tests but do not provide X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, or Ultrasounds. For advanced imaging, we can refer you to the appropriate facility.

 Do you offer prenatal and postnatal care? 

We do not provide prenatal or postnatal care services at our clinics.

 Can I get nutritional counseling at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, nutritional counseling is available from our general physicians.

 Can I get travel health advice and vaccinations at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, travel health advice is available. We can advise on required vaccinations and administer vaccines that you bring in.

 Are occupational health services available at Clinikk clinics? 

Occupational health services are not currently available at our clinics.

 Do you offer pediatric services at Clinikk? 

Yes, our general physicians are trained to provide care for children as well.

 Can I fill prescriptions on-site at Clinikk clinics? 

Yes, you can fill prescriptions on-site, but only for medications prescribed by a Clinikk doctor.

 Is telemedicine available for those who cannot visit the clinic? 

Yes, telemedicine services are available 24/7 through the Clinikk app for consultations with our general physicians.
