### Experienced and Qualified Doctors at Your Service

check-ups, chronic disease management, and preventive health needs. From a quick doctor consultation to ongoing care
for complex conditions, our general physicians provide personalised care based on your symptoms, history, and health
goals.

### Comprehensive Healthcare Services

Clinikk clinics are equipped to manage a wide range of everyday health concerns. Common conditions and services
include:

* Acute conditions: fever, cold, cough, sore throat, headaches, and body aches.
* Chronic diseases: diabetes, hypertension, and thyroid disorders.
* Respiratory infections and seasonal illnesses.
* Skin concerns such as allergic reactions, dermatitis, and fungal infections.
* Infections, including viral infections, urinary tract infections (UTI), and wound infections.
* Pain management for joint pain, back pain, and body aches.
* Dengue management with appropriate medical evaluation and follow-up.
* Lab testing services, including common blood tests that support diagnosis and treatment planning.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
---------------------------------

What types of medical consultations are available at Clinikk Health Hubs?

We offer consultations for common conditions like fever, body aches, diabetes, and more, with general physicians trained in family medicine, primary care, and urgent care.

 Can I receive vaccinations at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, select adult vaccinations are available at our Health Hubs, including HPV, typhoid, chickenpox, and flu vaccines. A consultation with a Clinikk doctor is required except when we are only administering a vaccine that was prescribed and obtained outside Clinikk.

 Do you offer allergy testing and treatment at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, allergy testing and treatment are provided at our Health Hubs.

 Can I get a health screening done at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, we offer health screenings, including blood tests, cholesterol checks, and other basic lab tests.

 What types of diagnostic imaging do you provide?

We do not offer diagnostic imaging such as X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, or ultrasounds. Basic lab tests and health screenings are available at Clinikk clinics.

 Do Clinikk clinics provide dermatological services?

No, specialist dermatology consultations are not available at our Health Hubs.

 Are mental health services available at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, our physicians can provide initial support for mental health concerns and refer patients to mental health professionals when needed.

 Can I receive treatment for chronic diseases at Clinikk clinics?

We provide management plans for chronic conditions like diabetes and hypertension, with support for medication management, lifestyle advice, and regular monitoring.

 How do I book an appointment at Clinikk clinics?

You can book an appointment by visiting clinikk.com or by calling your nearest Clinikk Health Hub.

 What are the clinic timings and days?

Our clinics operate from Monday to Saturday with the following timings:

* Morning: 9:30 AM-1:00 PM
* Evening: 4:00 PM-8:30 PM

All Clinikk clinics are closed on Sundays. Operating hours may vary by location, so please call the respective Clinikk Health Hub before visiting.

 What types of lab tests can be done at your Health Hub?

We offer common blood tests and basic lab tests. We do not provide X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, or ultrasounds.

 Do you offer prenatal and postnatal care?

No, we do not provide prenatal or postnatal care services at our clinics.

 Can I get nutritional counselling at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, nutritional counselling is available from our general physicians.

 Can I get travel health advice and vaccinations at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, travel health advice and select adult vaccinations are available at our Health Hubs.

 Are occupational health services available at Clinikk clinics?

No, occupational health services are not available at our clinics at this time.

 Do you offer paediatric services at Clinikk?

Yes, our general physicians are trained to provide care for children as well.

 Can I fill prescriptions on-site at Clinikk clinics?

Yes, you can fill prescriptions on-site, but only for medications prescribed by a Clinikk doctor.

 Is telemedicine available for those who cannot visit the clinic?

Yes, telemedicine services are available 24/7 through our website for consultations with our general physicians.
